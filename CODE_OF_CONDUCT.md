# Code of conduct

Meerko follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and
publishing other people's private information are not tolerated anywhere in the project's spaces.

Report a problem to contact@meerko.ai. Reports are read by the maintainers, kept confidential, and answered.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that break this code.
